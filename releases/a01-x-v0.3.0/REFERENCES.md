# A-01 X v0.3.0 — Reference Register

**Research snapshot:** 22 November 2026. Verify the applicable edition, jurisdiction and scope before using a source in engineering design. Standards may be copyrighted; this register links to official summaries and does not reproduce their normative text.

## Indoor environment and wellbeing

1. **ASHRAE Standard 55-2023 — Thermal Environmental Conditions for Human Occupancy.** Temperature, thermal radiation, humidity, air speed and personal activity/clothing factors are assessed together; the official overview notes vertical air-temperature gradient and local discomfort.  
   https://www.ashrae.org/technical-resources/bookstore/standard-55-thermal-environmental-conditions-for-human-occupancy
2. **ASHRAE Standards 62.1 & 62.2 — Ventilation and Indoor Air Quality.** Official overview of ventilation minimums, filtration, controls and operations; the 62.1-2025 overview highlights additional humidity, air-density, demand-control and emergency-control requirements.  
   https://ashrae.org/technical-resources/bookstore/standards-62-1-62-2
3. **WELL v2 — ten concepts.** Air, Water, Nourishment, Light, Movement, Thermal Comfort, Sound, Materials, Mind and Community. Used as a design checklist reference, not proof of certification or health outcome.  
   https://www.wellcertified.com/certification/v2-pilot/
4. **CIE PS 001:2024 — Position Statement on Integrative Lighting, 3rd edition.** Recommends the CIE system for metrology of ipRGC-influenced effects (CIE S 026:2018) while noting ongoing research questions.  
   https://www.cie.co.at/publications/cie-position-statement-integrative-lighting-recommending-proper-light-proper-time-3rd
5. **WHO Environmental Noise Guidelines for the European Region (2018).** Public-health guidance on environmental noise; apply the relevant local acoustic rules to the actual building use.  
   https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789289053563

## Digital infrastructure and operations

6. **NIST SP 800-82 Rev. 3 — Guide to Operational Technology (OT) Security (2023).** Final guidance for OT, including building automation systems.  
   https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/82/r3/final
7. **NIST SP 800-82 Rev. 4 Initial Public Draft (21 September 2026).** A draft was published for public comment through 30 November 2026. It is not a final replacement for Rev. 3 as of this research snapshot.  
   https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/82/r4/ipd

## Open modelling and information standards

8. **EnergyPlus Runtime API, version 26.2 documentation.** Documents callbacks and sensor/actuator access within a running EnergyPlus simulation; it is not a live BMS connection by itself.  
   https://energyplus.readthedocs.io/en/v26.2.0/runtime.html
9. **Radiance, U.S. Department of Energy overview.** Open-source lighting simulation software for calculating lighting metrics and rendering scenes.  
   https://www.energy.gov/cmei/buildings/articles/radiance
10. **IFC 4.3.2.0, buildingSMART International.** Open, vendor-neutral digital description standard for built assets; the page identifies the current official release as IFC 4.3.2.0.  
    https://www.buildingsmart.org/standards/bsi-standards/industry-foundation--classes/
11. **Information Delivery Specification (IDS), buildingSMART International.** Machine-interpretable exchange requirements for checking IFC information.  
    https://www.buildingsmart.org/standards/bsi-standards/information-delivery-specification-ids/
12. **ISO 19650-1:2018.** Concepts and principles for information management across the built-asset lifecycle; verify revision status before project procurement.  
    https://www.iso.org/standard/68078.html

## Use restrictions

- This register is a starting point, not a complete code compliance matrix.
- The project location and governing jurisdiction must be selected before assigning mandatory limits.
- Use the full licensed editions and qualified professional interpretation for design and compliance.
- A simulation result is only as reliable as its inputs, assumptions, boundary conditions and validation.
- A cited standard or tool does not imply that A-01 X has implemented, passed or been certified against it.
