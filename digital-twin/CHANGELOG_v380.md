# Changelog — A-01 v3.8.0

**Date:** 21 November 2026

## Added
- Standalone Three.js interior blockout inspired by the spatial composition of the Axiom ship in WALL‑E.
- Five conceptual levels L00–L04 with selectable and isolatable floor rings.
- Open central atrium, ring galleries, radial room blocks and cross-gallery bridges.
- Illustrative lift core, outer service ring, vertical riser concepts and planted pockets.
- Layer visibility controls and Atrium / Top / Entry camera presets.
- RU, EN and ZH documentation.

## Preserved
- v3.7.0 remains unchanged as the latest integrated digital-twin build.
- v3.8.0 is a companion page linked from the digital-twin index; its geometry is not merged into the v3.7.0 main scene.

## Known limitations
- All geometry uses uncalibrated scene units, not metres.
- The scene is an architectural massing study, not BIM or construction documentation.
- No structural, accessibility, fire, HVAC, acoustic, plumbing, lift traffic or evacuation calculations are performed.
- Lift-car motion is a visual placeholder only.
- Requires WebGL and internet access to load Three.js 0.160.0 from jsDelivr.
- No Pixar/Disney or third-party model geometry is embedded; Axiom is used as a design-research reference only.
