# A-01 Project Charter

## Mission

Develop a human-centred concept for an environment connecting architecture, energy, digital infrastructure, knowledge and AI-assisted services.

## Foundational principles

- The Sphere must outlive its founder.
- The project exists to serve people, not to concentrate power in one person or system.
- EVA CARE should be accessible and should connect people to human support.
- Critical decisions require accountable human oversight.
- Knowledge, records and operational procedures must be transferable across generations.

## Charter statement

Ева не принадлежит одному человеку. Сфера создаётся для людей. Её задача — сохранять знания, помогать каждому и передавать будущее следующим поколениям.

## Truth statement

A-01 is not a promise that something already exists. It is an open record of the work toward what we hope to make real.
