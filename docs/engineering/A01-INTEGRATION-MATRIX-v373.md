# A-01 — Engineering Integration Matrix
**Documentation package v3.7.3 · 21 November 2026 · EN**

> Status: working engineering concept. This document connects the v3.7.2 digital-twin line, the supplied v3.1.6 Gull-wing / Crown calculations and the engineering requirements. It is not construction, installation, permit or commissioned-system documentation.

## 1. Summary and maturity labels

The five workstreams are retained: **A01 Engineering Core**, **A02 Climate & Crown**, **A03 Serviceable Shell**, **A04 Energy & Resilience**, and **A05 Smart Operations**. HTML scenes and switches are demonstrators only. No physical sensors, actuators, BMS/PLC, mobile identity system or real loads are connected.

Maturity labels: **CONCEPT** (idea/visual model); **MODEL** (software logic or scenario calculator with uncalibrated inputs); **ENGINEERING REQUIRED** (site data, calculations, qualified design and code review required); **VERIFIED** (only with traceable test evidence or independent engineering sign-off). No item in this matrix is marked verified.

## 3. Critical engineering corrections and open questions

### Crown, ventilation and smoke
The v3.1.6 calculator uses simplified dome-volume and outdoor-air assumptions. It does not establish duct, shaft, damper or zone performance. Verify each louvre's free area and discharge coefficient, screens/filters, wind pressure and duct losses. A visual smoke mode is not proof of smoke control. A separate smoke/egress design must calculate smoke layer, temperature, evacuation, free area and make-up air. Fire control and power must be independent of Eva and cloud services.

### Thermal and energy model
The original envelope heat-loss calculation uses a fixed 30 °C temperature difference. Replace it with scenario-specific indoor/outdoor design temperatures and include U-values, surface areas, thermal bridges, infiltration and operating schedules. The illustrative solar-hour assumptions (5 h summer / 1 h winter) are not a site yield forecast or bankable energy estimate. Use site coordinates, horizon, orientation, snow, shading, module temperature and system losses. Separate electrical, heating and cooling balances; define critical loads and autonomy. Server heat is not a guaranteed heating source.

### Moving leaves and modules
Treat v3.1.6 inputs such as module/leaf areal mass, speed, wind and safety factor as unverified model assumptions. Check static/dynamic loads, wind, snow, fatigue, anchorage, deflection, vibration, corrosion, thermal movement, icing, pinch/crush hazards, falling objects and jammed mechanisms. Specify mechanical catches, independent brakes, position feedback, manual recovery and exclusion-zone interlocks. Remote movement start must be prohibited while anyone is in the hazard zone; require local authorization and verified zone clearance. Full occupied-module extraction and exterior lowering remain research concepts, not verified v3.7.2 functionality.

### Phone access and cyber security
A01-SEC-01 is currently a local browser simulation: no real pairing, backend, IAM, controller, relay, sensors or physical loads are connected. Never expose controllers directly to the internet. Use a secure gateway, OT/IT segmentation, MFA/passkeys, short-lived signed commands, session revocation, audit and local interlocks. A bulk-off command must never include fire alarm, emergency lighting, smoke control, emergency communications or critical pumps. Show actual feedback separately from command acceptance.

## 4. Integration sequence

1. **E0 — Basis of design:** select site/climate, design scenarios, geometry and system boundaries.
2. **E1 — Requirements and assets:** IDs, passports, criticality, interfaces, cause/effect and maintenance requirements.
3. **E2 — Independent safety concepts:** fire, egress, airlocks, moving parts, electrical isolation and manual recovery.
4. **E3 — Engineering models:** ventilation/smoke, thermal balance, energy, shell loads, kinematics and failure cases.
5. **E4 — Prototypes and tests:** physical leaf/seal/cassette sample, controller bench, failure testing and mobile UX validation.
6. **E5 — Digital-twin integration:** show provenance, units, ranges and explicit “model/measured” labels.
7. **E6 — Independent review:** qualified designers, regulatory review and test evidence before any physical implementation decision.

## 5. Codes and professional review

For a Russian project, verify the current applicable editions and scope of fire protection, smoke control, ventilation, egress, structures, electrical installations, automation, lifts/hoists, accessibility and sanitation requirements. СП 7.13130.2013 and its amendments are an initial reference for heating, ventilation and smoke protection; the designer and fire-safety specialist must confirm current applicability. NFPA 92 (2024) may be supplementary reference material, not a substitute for mandatory Russian requirements. Do not claim code compliance without documented review.

## 6. Documentation acceptance criteria

Every system has an owner, purpose, ID, boundary and interfaces. Every number has a source, unit, scenario and provenance (input/calculated/measured). Normal, failure, emergency and manual modes are defined. Protected circuits and phone/Eva permissions are explicit. Calculations are reproducible and uncertainties visible. “Verified” requires actual test reports or independent sign-off.
