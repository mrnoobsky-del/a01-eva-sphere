# A-01 v3.7.0 — Gull-wing and Crown integration

## Scope

This integration branch is based on the published A-01 v3.6.0 AIR-01 digital twin. It retains the AIR-01 layer and adds two conceptual subsystems from the supplied v3.1.6 prototype:

- **A01-CV-01 — Crown ventilation:** 24 adjustable louvres, a lifting rain hood, six visual exhaust fans, an interior exhaust ring and three conceptual duct connections.
- **A01-GW-01 — Gull-wing service access:** an upper-hinged access concept, paired actuator struts, an inner vestibule gate and a short service tray. A 12-step visual service sequence is included.

## Important boundary

The Gull-wing demonstrator in v3.7.0 models access and service clearance only. It does not extract a complete occupied module, lower it along the dome, or claim that the structure is mechanically safe. Those operations remain a separate engineering work package.

The Crown controls and calculations are scenario tools. They are not a commissioned HVAC system, smoke-control design, CFD result, fire strategy or measured telemetry. Fire-mode animation is a visual demonstration only; actual smoke control must be designed and activated by an independent code-compliant fire safety system.

## Crown scenario calculation

The preliminary calculator preserves the supplied prototype's simplifying assumptions and equations:
- dome volume approximated as an ellipsoidal hemisphere;
- natural stack flow from a simplified pressure/temperature relation;
- effective inlet area from crown and lower openings;
- a simplified axisymmetric smoke-plume estimate.

The default scenario remains 2.4 m per model unit, 6 m crown diameter, 55% free louvre area, 30 m² lower inlet area, 22 °C indoor air, 16 °C night air, −30 °C winter air and a 2.5 MW design fire. Calculations are intentionally labelled preliminary.

## Safety requirements before engineering adoption

1. Separate normal ventilation faults from fire-mode activation. Loss of power must not automatically open the crown during winter.
2. Use independently verified fail-safe dampers, position feedback, weather/icing interlocks and a safe manual recovery path.
3. Interlock the two vestibule boundaries: never permit both doors to be open together; confirm pressure equalization before releasing the inner boundary.
4. For the moving module concept, verify wind and snow loads, hinge fatigue, actuator redundancy, centre of gravity, arrestors, rail buckling, emergency braking, icing, drainage, seals and rescue access.
5. Validate the full assembly with structural calculations, wind analysis, HVAC design, CFD smoke-control analysis, evacuation review and independent professional review.
