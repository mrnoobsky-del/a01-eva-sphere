# Status and evidence

Use explicit status labels throughout the repository:

- **Concept** — an idea or proposed direction; not implemented.
- **Research** — information gathering and preliminary analysis.
- **Prototype** — an experimental implementation; not necessarily production-ready.
- **Verified** — checked against stated criteria, with evidence recorded.
- **Deployed** — released into a real environment; deployment evidence and date must be stated.

Do not describe concept imagery as a photograph of a completed facility. Do not present preliminary calculations as certified engineering design. Every claim about real-world operation must have traceable evidence.
